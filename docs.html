How Everpool works.

Everpool is a launchpad built purely around the pool. Launch a genuine pools.trade token, and every trade feeds a permanently-locked pool while the creator's fees are burned. Here's the whole thing, end to end.

01

What Everpool is

Everpool is a token launchpad on Robinhood Chain that launches real pools.trade tokens. Where most launchpads let value leak to the founder, Everpool routes it all at the pool: the bulk of every fee compounds back into locked liquidity, and the creator's share is burned instead of pocketed.

The ambition is simple — a launchpad that cares about one thing, the pool, so it can only ever get deeper. A constant snowball.

02

The pool engine (the snowball)

Every token launched through Everpool trades in its own Uniswap-v4 pool on pools.trade. That pool charges a small fee on every swap. Instead of going to a wallet, the bulk of that fee auto-compounds straight back into the pool — paired up and re-added as locked liquidity.

  • A trade pays a fee to the pool.
  • The fee is paired and re-pooled — permanently locked.
  • A deeper pool means tighter prices and more volume — which means more fees. Then it repeats.
The pool is single-sided and permanently locked. Nobody — founder included — can pull the liquidity. It can only grow.
03

The burn: dev fees are destroyed

pools.trade splits trading fees between the pool (the majority, which autocompounds) and the token's creator. On Everpool, that creator share never reaches a person. It accrues to the Everpool BurnVault, and when it's claimed it is sent to the dead address 0x…dEaD — removed from supply forever.

The claim is permissionless: anyone can trigger the burn (a keeper does it automatically). So there is no dev cut, ever — the incentives point entirely at the token and its pool.

04

Genuine pools.trade tokens

Everpool doesn't fork or imitate pools.trade — it launches through pools.trade's official LiquidityLauncherV3. Your token is a real pools.trade token, tradable on pools.trade, with the same locked-liquidity and autocompounding mechanics. Everpool's contribution is the fee recipient: it burns the creator share.

05

Launching a token

Launching is single-sided, so there's no capital to seed — just gas.

  • Open the launch page and connect your wallet (MetaMask on Robinhood Chain).
  • Enter a name, ticker and optional logo.
  • Sign one transaction. Your token deploys, all supply is seeded into a locked pool, and it goes live on pools.trade.
  • Find it under Discover; open it to see supply, CA and a link to its live chart.
You sign every step in your own wallet. Everpool never touches your keys.

FAQ

Does the creator earn anything?

No. The creator's fee share is burned when claimed. The point of Everpool is that nobody extracts value — it all serves the pool and the holders.

Is the liquidity locked?

Yes, permanently and single-sided. It can't be withdrawn by anyone, including the founder. It can only grow as fees compound in.

Is it a real pools.trade token?

Yes. Everpool launches through pools.trade's official LiquidityLauncherV3, so your token is genuine and trades on pools.trade.

What does it cost to launch?

Only gas. Launching is single-sided, so you don't need to provide any capital to seed the pool.

Where do I see price and market cap?

Live price, chart and market cap run on pools.trade. Each token's page on Everpool links straight to it, plus the block explorer.

Who can trigger the burn?

Anyone — the burn is permissionless. A keeper runs it automatically when it's worth the gas.
